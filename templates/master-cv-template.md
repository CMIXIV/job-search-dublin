# Master CV template

Source of truth. Never sent anywhere. Tailored versions are cut down from this.

Keep every provable claim here, even the ones that will not fit on a two-page
tailored CV - the point of the master is that nothing has to be remembered twice.

Unknowns stay as `[CAPITALISED PLACEHOLDERS]` until the person supplies them.
Never fill one in with a plausible guess.

---

# [FULL NAME]

**[Current or target title]** | [City, Country] | [PHONE] | [EMAIL] | [linkedin.com/in/HANDLE]

## Profile

Three or four lines. The distinctive combination - the two or three things that
are rare *together* - with the two biggest numbers in it. Written for the target
role, not as a career summary.

> Example shape (fictional): *Product leader across payments, risk and applied ML.
> Owned fraud detection for a platform processing EUR 4bn a year in 14 markets, and
> built a data ingestion capability from zero to 200TB. Twelve years across
> fintech, retail and logistics.*

## Skills

Group them. Four to six groups, comma-separated inside each. Include the exact
words the target roles use - a recruiter greps this section in six seconds.

- **Domain:** [pricing, marketplace, payments...]
- **Technical:** [SQL, Python, ML concepts, experimentation...]
- **Product:** [discovery, roadmapping, pricing strategy, platform...]
- **Leadership:** [team size, cross-functional, hiring, mentoring...]
- **Tools:** [only the ones that matter for the target]

## Experience

### [Company] - [Title]
*[Month Year] - [Month Year] | [Location]*

One line of context: what the company does and the scope of the role, so a
reader who has never heard of the employer can calibrate.

- Outcome-first bullet: verb, what changed, by how much, over what scope.
- Each bullet carries a number or a named artefact. If neither exists yet, mark
  it `[NEEDS NUMBER]`.
- Four to six bullets for the most recent role, fewer as you go back.
