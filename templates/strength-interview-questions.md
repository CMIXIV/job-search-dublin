# Strength interview - question bank

For phase 1. Ask one at a time, follow the thread, push for a number. Ten to
fifteen of these is a session; do not run the whole bank.

## Scope and scale

1. What is the biggest thing - money, users, headcount, data, markets - that
   depended on a decision you personally made?
2. What was the largest budget or revenue line you were accountable for?
3. How many people worked on what you owned? How many reported to you?
4. What was the scale of the system, market or portfolio you were responsible for?

## Outcomes

5. What did you ship that is still running?
6. What is the one thing in the last three years the company could not have done
   without you?
7. What did you inherit broken, and what state was it in when you left?
8. What did you kill, and what did that save?
9. What did you build, hire, or start from zero?
10. What is the number you were most judged on, and what did it do while you were
    there?

## Influence

11. When did you change your organisation's mind about something?
12. What do colleagues come to you for that is not in your job description?
13. Who did you make better at their job?
14. What did you have to get agreement on across teams that did not report to you?
15. What decision did you make that was unpopular and correct?

## Distinctiveness

16. What can you do that most people with your title cannot?
17. Which two or three of your skills are rare *together*?
18. What would your last manager name as your strongest achievement? Would you
    name the same one?
19. What do you get asked to fix when it is urgent?

## The hard ones

20. What is the hardest part of your job that you have made look easy?
21. What have you failed at, and what did you change afterwards?
22. What is the gap in your profile that a good interviewer will find?
23. What would make you turn down an offer?

## Follow-ups that get the number

- "From what to what, over how long?"
- "How do you know it was you and not something else happening at the same time?"
- "What would have happened if you had not done it?"
- "Who else would claim credit for that, and what did you specifically own?"
- "Is that documented anywhere - a review, a deck, a post-mortem?"

## Listen for the undersell

- "We" where they mean "I"
- "Helped with" where they led
- "Just" anything
- Eleven years compressed into one line
- The achievement mentioned in passing and then dropped - that one is frequently
  the strongest material in the entire conversation
